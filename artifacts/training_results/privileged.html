Controlled diagnostic

Both actor and critic are feedforward 256×256 MLPs, using normalized privileged state. There are no RGB encoders or camera renders. PPO uses std1, entropy0.01, adaptive LR0.0003, four epochs/eight minibatches, 2,048 worlds and24 steps/update. Actions, rewards, geometry, 12s horizon, dynamic optical panel and ±20mm reset jitter match the halted RGB runs.

Each task starts from scratch at training seed0. Evaluations near2M,5M and10M transitions use512 episodes at reset seeds30000 and31000. The initial diagnostic is capped at10,027,008 transitions per task. Any reviewed extension is recorded below; no automatic RGB submission follows. At least90% success on both final validation sets is the provisional competence criterion.

This changes the actor architecture as well as its information. It checks reward/control/task learnability; it is not a matched RGB-versus-state benchmark. Failure with privileged state would motivate reward, action-space or optimization diagnosis. Success would justify proceeding to visual learning.

Transfer must physically grasp and lift, then support/release/settle and withdraw for five samples. Push must meet goal tolerances, withdraw the actual pushing tip, lose manipulator contact and settle for three samples. Reward increase alone does not pass the gate.

Interpretation and next action

Transfer at 20,004,864 transitions: 0/512 / 0/512. No held-out completions. Reward growth alone does not establish task learnability.

Push at 20,004,864 transitions: 501/512 / 510/512. Meets the provisional 90% competence criterion on both sets. Review trajectories before RGB training.

The initial 10M diagnostics are complete. At 2M and 5M, both tasks scored zero successes on both 512-episode held-out validation sets.

At 10,027,008 transitions, push scored 361/512 (70.5%) and 345/512 (67.4%) on the two held-out sets, versus zero at 5M. Actual evaluated success and failure trajectories were captured and rendered. This establishes substantial learning under the existing recipe, but does not meet the provisional ≥90% competence criterion.

Transfer scored 0/512 and 0/512 at 10M; all failures lacked the required physical lift history. Its training reward increased from approximately 21 at 2M to 69 at 10M while full task success remained zero. This is a concrete warning against treating reward growth as task competence. Bilateral grasps and partial lifts are developing, so a bounded extension can test whether they lead to the next stage.

The matched-reset physical reward audit favors scripted completion over each tested stalled controller for both total and discounted return. Transfer completion averaged 37.99 discounted return, versus 12.16 for reaching only, 13.15 for grasping without lifting, and 23.01 for lifting without placing. Push completion averaged 41.79, versus 11.41 for remaining behind the object and 18.65 for the controller without explicit withdrawal. This is a limited reward sanity check, not evidence of learned competence.

Both tasks have one reviewed extension to 20M cumulative transitions, using unchanged rewards, controls and PPO settings. Neither passed the initial competence gate. RGB remains stopped; its eventual per-run minimum remains 1B transitions.
