Old high success rates are not a clean target baseline. The legacy task README marks the 94.0% wrist / 86.2% static / 100% active seed-0 results superseded: variant-dependent mass and inertia produced a sag signal that policies could use. Its active policy still scored 97.4% with camera motion frozen. These are historical documented figures, not newly reproduced measurements. Read the source.

The proposed 80–90% static and 90–100% active targets remain hypotheses. Neither this repair nor the older results establish that ordering under matched geometry, held-out starts, training budgets and robustness tests.

The old reward was materially different

A hard XM approach failure has a concrete explanation

Native MuJoCo, seed 10011, unchanged target and three translation actions. The original controller moves horizontally while lifting; a prong catches the socket. Its commanded pose is reachable to numerical precision, but contact prevents the physical arm from following.

At the jam, prong-to-socket contacts carry 7.49 N and 6.50 N normal forces. Holding XY while first lifting the TCP to 90 mm removes this failure, then the same approach/insert controller succeeds. This is a one-case privileged diagnostic, not a learned-policy improvement or a new 512-episode success rate. The production scripted controller and training reset were not changed by this diagnostic.

The learned-policy evidence is different: seven XM episodes briefly reach <2 mm but do not hold for three samples; median closest XM error is 2.248 mm. Near-contact alignment and stable insertion remain unresolved. Full contact and IK measurements.

Implemented and checked

  • Optional wrist yaw: a fourth manipulator command, bounded to ±30° with a 3°/step rate limit. It uses the existing arm joints and respects the one-second inspection pause.
  • Orientation-aware success: yaw runs require physical orientation error <2° as well as <2 mm position error for three samples. The evaluator retains all three orientation measurements. Position-only historical checkpoints retain their original schema.
  • Orientation shaping: yaw runs add an angle cost with a 30 mm lever-arm scale to the log reward. This means the pair tests a usable yaw mode, including its scoring/shaping, rather than isolating action dimension alone.
  • Full feedforward recipe: both actor and critic are feedforward; simply setting the old --memory none left the critic recurrent. Resume validation protects architecture, action dimensions and objective.
  • Validation: 166 non-GPU regression tests passed, then 15 orientation/control checks and 71 targeted tests after adding configuration preservation and checkpoint save/load coverage. Native tests rotate and realign the physical plug and insert all four variants. GPU validation status: complete; passed artifact: True.

Bounded experiment and decision rule

Status: complete. Slurm job: 51624849. Two fresh seed-0 fixed-view-7 runs, one GPU sequentially, each 751 updates × 512 environments × 24 steps = 9,228,288 transitions. Total: 18,456,576. No automatic budget extension or active/wrist sweep.

Both runs use the recovered feedforward/log recipe. The second enables yaw plus orientation scoring/shaping. Each final checkpoint receives two 512-episode physical validations: 128 episodes for each variant, reset seeds 10000–10003. Representative trajectories and videos are captured. The competence gate is at least 103/128 successes for every variant in both repeats.
